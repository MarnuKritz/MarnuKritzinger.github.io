Professional Experience

Electrical Technician

IMPACT Services Group Ltd.

From April to August 2025, I worked as an Electrical Technician at IMPACT Services Group, assisting with electrical installations, fibre-optic cabling, and HVAC control systems across residential, commercial, and university buildings. This role gave me practical experience working with electrical hardware, troubleshooting installations, and integrating control components in real-world environments.

Electrical Installation HVAC Controls Fibre-Optic Cabling Testing & Troubleshooting Systems Integration Worksite Safety

April 2025 – August 2025

01 — Electrical Installation

Electrical Systems & Controls

A major part of my role involved assisting with the installation and setup of electrical components across a variety of active worksites. I independently installed and configured HVAC controls, including thermostats, switches, timers, and AC actuator controls, as well as high-to-low voltage transformer units, allowing other team members to focus on additional tasks.

This gave me practical experience working with electrical connections and control hardware, while reinforcing the importance of accurate installation, reliable operation, and following appropriate safety procedures.

02 — Cabling & Testing

Installation & System Reliability

I also assisted with the installation and testing of fibre-optic and electrical cabling. This involved working across residential homes, offices, university buildings, and active construction sites, adapting to different installation requirements and coordinating with the rest of the team.

These responsibilities contributed to improvements in setup and inspection efficiency, with a reported reduction in time of approximately 20–30%. The experience helped me develop a better understanding of practical testing, installation quality, and the challenges of integrating electrical systems into existing infrastructure.

03 — Field Experience

Working Safely in Real Environments

Working across different sites taught me to account for the practical constraints that come with electrical installation, from existing building infrastructure to coordinating work around other trades. I followed applicable installation and worksite safety requirements while carrying out assigned tasks.

This experience strengthened my ability to work independently, contribute to a team, and approach electrical hardware with attention to detail and reliability.

Engineering Takeaways

What I Took Away

This role gave me a practical foundation in electrical installation and control systems that complements my academic background in electrical circuits and electromagnetics. It also reinforced how important reliable connections, proper testing, and safe working practices are when integrating electrical components into larger systems — skills I continue to apply in my robotics and UAV work.
