Problem & Objectives

02 — MECHANICAL DESIGN

Airframe Design & Fabrication

Describe your contribution to the design and manufacture of the primary multirotor airframe. Discuss the structural layout, component mounting, packaging constraints, and the design decisions that influenced the physical assembly.

Explain how you took the design from CAD or initial concepts into manufactured hardware. Include the materials, manufacturing methods, and assembly techniques you actually used.

03 — SYSTEMS INTEGRATION

Avionics & Hardware Integration

Describe your contribution to the mechanical provisions and hardware used to integrate avionics and supporting electronics into the aircraft. Explain how the arrangement accommodated the surrounding mechanical systems.

Discuss the practical considerations involved in mounting components, routing connections, managing space, and making the integrated system accessible for assembly or maintenance, where applicable.

04 — MECHANICAL DEVELOPMENT

Magnetic Switch Mechanism

Explain the purpose of the magnetic-switch mechanism and the requirements it needed to meet. Describe how you approached the mechanical design and selected the arrangement of its components.

Discuss the design trade-offs you considered, how the mechanism was manufactured or assembled, and how you assessed its operation. Include the actual design constraints and testing methods relevant to your work.

05 — VALIDATION

Testing & Iteration

Describe the checks or tests you performed on the mechanical components and integrated hardware. Explain what you were trying to verify and how you assessed whether the design met its requirements.

Where applicable, discuss an issue you encountered, how you investigated its cause, and what changes you made. Be specific about measured results or observed improvements when you have evidence to support them.

06 — OUTCOME

Results & Engineering Takeaways

Summarize what you completed, the systems you contributed to, and the requirements or milestones you were able to demonstrate. Include concrete performance results only where you have supporting evidence.

Reflect briefly on what the project taught you about mechanical design, fabrication, and integrating hardware into a complex aerospace system.
