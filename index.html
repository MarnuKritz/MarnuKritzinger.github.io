MECHANICAL DESIGN | MAGNETISM

Mechanical On/Off Magnet

Ongoing

One of my first major projects for UBC UAS, I designed a permanent magnet that could be turned on and off by a simple rotation motion, rather than utilizing electronics or circuits to do so. Designed to be used on a Mothership VTOL Drone to latch a subdrone firmly onto the mothership to be carried over long distances. Project is in its final testing/validation phases, though now as a personal project.

I built a fully discrete analog and digital based closed-loop motor speed controller using optical sensing, digital counting, amplifiers, R2R ladders, and more to hold a DC motor at a potentiometer-set speed.
